# Store Screenshots

To submit to the Chrome Web Store, you need to capture these screenshots manually since the local environment cannot capture real browser renders.

Required format: 1280x800 or 640x400 pixels, JPEG or PNG.

### Screenshot 1: Highlight Save (The Core Loop)
- **URL**: Any interesting article (e.g., Wikipedia or a blog).
- **Action**: Highlight a paragraph of text.
- **Visibility**: The floating "Save to Forgot AI" button appearing near the highlighted text.
- **Caption Suggestion**: "Highlight to save anything instantly."

### Screenshot 2: Content Save (Mode B)
- **URL**: ChatGPT, Claude, or X/Twitter.
- **Action**: Hover over a specific AI response or tweet.
- **Visibility**: The native-looking "Save to Forgot AI" button seamlessly injected at the bottom of the response.
- **Caption Suggestion**: "One-click save for AI chats and social posts."

### Screenshot 3: Saving / Success State
- **URL**: Same as Screenshot 1.
- **Action**: After clicking the Save button.
- **Visibility**: The UI showing the green checkmark "✓ Saved to Forgot AI".
- **Caption Suggestion**: "Frictionless capture without leaving your workflow."

### Screenshot 4: Extension Popup / Search
- **URL**: Any page.
- **Action**: Open the extension side panel or popup.
- **Visibility**: The sleek search pill and chat interface retrieving a past memory.
- **Caption Suggestion**: "Retrieve your memories with natural language."
