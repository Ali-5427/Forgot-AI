# Store Listing: Forgot AI

## Extension Name
Forgot AI

## Short Description
Save highlights, notes, links, and articles to your personal memory vault in one click.

## Full Description
Forgot AI is your personal, frictionless memory vault. Save useful content while you browse, forget about organizing it, and retrieve it later instantly with AI semantic search.

Whether you're researching, reading long articles, or capturing ideas, Forgot AI captures the context seamlessly so you never lose an important thought again.

## Key Features
- **One-Click Highlight Save**: Simply highlight any text on any webpage and click "Save to Forgot AI" to instantly capture it along with its source URL and page title.
- **Smart Side Panel**: Access all your saved memories right inside your browser without leaving your active tab.
- **Zero Organization Needed**: You don't need to categorize, tag, or folder your saves. The backend AI automatically summarizes, tags, and organizes everything for you.
- **Semantic Retrieval**: Find any saved memory by simply asking for it. Forgot AI searches by meaning, not just exact keywords.
- **Privacy First**: Your memories are isolated to your account. The extension requests minimal permissions, never monitors your background browsing, and never injects third-party trackers or remote executable code.

## How it works
1. **Highlight & Save**: Select text anywhere on the web to see the minimal floating save button.
2. **Add Context**: Optionally type a quick note on why you saved it.
3. **Retrieve Later**: Open the side panel or the web app to search and chat with your memory vault.

## Supported Platforms
- Universal text highlight and save works on any website.
- Side panel memory search and chat available directly inside Chrome.
